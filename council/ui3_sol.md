V3 improves card names, rejection hierarchy, and result feedback. Krillion has the strongest visual world, but its tiny, dim text hurts readability.

Most impactful remaining problems, in order:

1. **Collection — repeated cards lose their meaning.** Several identical “Red standpipe · Common · 10” cards dominate the grid, with prompt context removed since v2. Group repeated finds from the same prompt/object into one tile with a count; retain the prompt when it distinguishes cards.

2. **Across the app — secondary information is too small and faint for outdoor use.** Attempts remaining, rarity counts, timers between rounds, and gray metadata require close inspection. Make actionable status text at least 14px at actual device size; darken muted text and the Common badge background.

3. **Round hunt — the active task sits below excessive empty space.** The timer and prompt feel disconnected. Move the prompt into the upper third beneath the timer; keep the camera action anchored at the bottom.

4. **Home — Collection is hard to discover.** The isolated four-square icon gives little indication that it opens your cards. Make the card fan a collection entry point and use a labeled “Collection” control alongside Today and Friends.

5. **Mobile landing/card footer — metadata still wraps awkwardly.** “One of One” and “Only find” compete inside the foreground card. Keep the tier pill on one line and remove the redundant “Only find” caption; apply the same footer layout throughout.

6. **Mobile landing — the explanation becomes a long stack of boxes.** Five tier descriptions followed by Daily and Squad dilute the strong opening. Reduce tiers to name, color, and points; present Daily and Squad as two compact mode rows.
