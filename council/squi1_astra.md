2. **Play—clear, impact, down:** Instructions become ambiguous. Replace “Probe 30 m south” with a direct action naming its destination. Replace “Brace” with the hit outcome and remaining lives. For downed players, show **“Wait for a teammate”**, nearest teammate distance, and progress only while revival is happening.

3. **Results:** “1 of 1 still standing” conflicts with three checked crew members. Calculate the count and row statuses consistently; use **“Your crew outlasted the storm”** when the team wins but the player remains down.

4. **Play—layout and controls:** A large empty middle separates radar from instructions, while the floating black circle has no obvious meaning. Bring navigation guidance closer to the radar; make the demo joystick recognizable with directional marks and show it only in demo mode.

5. **Mobile play / projector:** Teammate names, lives, and radar details are too faint or small for outdoor glances and distant viewing. Enlarge names and health indicators, strengthen essential strokes, and reduce decorative ticks. On the projector, replace seven equally weighted event lines with one prominent current event.
