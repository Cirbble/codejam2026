Current Rarefind improves action hierarchy, mobile landing composition, and result clarity. Krillion’s strongest advantage is how consistently its setting, typography, controls, and rewards reinforce one world.

Most impactful remaining problems, in order:

1. **Today / card detail — examples undermine the game’s premise.** The payphone does not visibly demonstrate “Something triangular”; the cardinal does not demonstrate “An interesting shadow.” Use photographs with unmistakable prompt matches. These examples teach players what counts.

2. **Across the app — small, faint text struggles against the outdoor use case.** Attempts remaining, card metadata, round transitions, and secondary actions are especially weak. Increase essential status text to roughly 14–16px at actual phone size, darken it, and remove expendable metadata.

3. **Orange buttons — white labels appear low contrast.** The broader use of orange improves consistency but loses the previous black buttons’ readability. Darken the orange until white labels meet contrast requirements, or use near-black labels and icons.

4. **Mobile landing — card artwork still breaks at narrow widths.** The foreground payphone title clips, and “Something red” wraps awkwardly inside card headers. Use a dedicated compact card layout with shorter display titles and reserved space for points; keep the front card completely readable.

5. **Squad card reveal — the reward has competing focal points.** The diagonal “COMMON” stamp covers the photograph and repeats the tier badge, while “+10 points” sits faintly underneath. Remove the stamp, enlarge the points beneath the card, and keep the tier in its existing badge.

6. **Home / squad lobby — empty space outweighs useful content.** The home card fan feels undersized; lobby membership is disconnected from the bottom controls. Enlarge and raise the home fan slightly; group squad code, invite action, and members more tightly, while retaining bottom-aligned Start.

7. **Collection — miniature card text creates visual noise.** Full card layouts become difficult to scan at two columns. Give collection thumbnails a simplified layout: photograph, readable object name, and tier; reserve prompt and find-count metadata for card detail.
