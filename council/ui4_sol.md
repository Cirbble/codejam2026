V4’s strongest improvements are the grouped collection, explicit Cards navigation, rejection retry button, and compact mobile tier list. Krillion’s advantage is how consistently its setting makes answering and scoring feel like one experience.

Most impactful remaining problems, in order:

1. **Card detail / find reveal — rarity needs a clearer comparison.** “Nobody else has found this” leaves “this” ambiguous; “3 of 3 finds” requires interpretation. Replace the existing explanation with one concrete statement: **“Only you found a pennant flag · 14 finds today”** or **“3 of 3 players found a standpipe.”** Use the actual scoring population.

2. **Today / collection / card detail — secondary text gets too small and faint.** Prompt metadata, colored tier names, and card footers lose readability at phone size, especially outdoors. Increase metadata size, darken colored text, and remove redundant text to make room. Keep “85 pts” together.

3. **Round hunt — the countdown lacks visual authority.** The tiny corner timer and thin bar are overwhelmed by the prompt card and empty space. Enlarge the timer, place it directly above the prompt, and strengthen the progress bar so remaining time is immediately readable.

4. **Home — completed finds outweigh the next action.** The card fan and score dominate while the unfinished prompt sits below them. Reduce the fan and give the next-prompt panel more space, with the entire panel acting as the camera entry point.

5. **Collection — full card layouts become cramped thumbnails.** Prompt headers truncate, and the duplicate badge overlaps the card’s upper edge. Use a compact collection variant: photo, readable object name, tier, points, and a reserved corner for the duplicate count. Keep the full layout for detail.

6. **Camera — prompt readability depends on the feed.** White text and the outlined close control lack a dependable backing. Put them over a dark top scrim and give Close a solid translucent background. The fake feed and test chips are excluded from this assessment.
