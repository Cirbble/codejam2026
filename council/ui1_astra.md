Pocket has no meaningful iconography to score. The competitors’ strongest advantage is that their characters and worlds dominate the experience.

Most impactful fixes, in order:

1. **08 — Rebuild the projector hierarchy.** At 1080p, use a 56px phase/objective heading, 48px countdown, and minimum 24px player names/verdicts. Zoom the map to the arena, suppress incidental map labels, remove the event log, and give the latest submitted photo a large, bright card.

2. **06 — Make escape direction visible.** Add a north marker to the radar and a thick arrow from “You” toward the safe exit. Put **“11 m · 8 s”** beside the arrow. Strengthen strike boundaries so they cannot be confused with distance rings.

3. **04/06 — Prioritize the current task and timer.** Increase the phase countdown to 28px beside “A shoe” or “Walk north-east.” Keep overall game time smaller in the top bar; move points out of the hunger panel.

4. **03/07 — Stop the footer covering content.** Reserve bottom scroll space equal to the entire fixed action area, plus safe-area inset and 16px. Keep sharing controls and awards fully reachable above it.

5. **04/06/07 — Bring Krillion into gameplay.** Attach the home screen’s pink mascot to the hunger meter, use an angry expression during Rage, and replace the results wave emoji with the same character. This is the clearest opportunity to make the game memorable.

6. **07 — Make the grass report the reward.** Lead with distance, finds, and submitted photos. Combine dodges/revives into one compact row; place the leaderboard afterward. Move “Demo” to a single badge so “Walked” does not wrap awkwardly.

7. **01–07 — Strengthen functional secondary text.** Use at least 14px for labels and inactive options, reduce wide letter spacing, and brighten muted blue-gray text. Prioritize lobby choices, radar labels, and pocket instructions.

8. **05 — Add a visible return action.** Place one outlined **“Back to hunt”** button at the bottom. Preserve the black background and generous empty space.

9. **01/02/03 — Simplify entry and setup.** Make “Join a squad” open a full-width code field; shorten how-to instructions to one sentence per mechanic while retaining safety/privacy information; replace mixed emoji with consistent icons; hide the lobby’s raw spectator URL behind **“Share big screen.”**
