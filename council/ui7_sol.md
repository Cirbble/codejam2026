Krillion’s strongest assets are its expressive mascot and unmistakable coral actions. Its remaining weakness is presentation: small supporting elements and oversized containers dilute those strengths, particularly on the projector.

Most impactful fixes, in order:

1. **Phone Rage — “Impact in 0 s.”** At zero, replace the countdown with **“Impact!”** and immediately transition to the resolved state. Never leave “Walk 13 m” displayed alongside an expired warning.

2. **Big-screen results — modal composition.** Remove the full-width “2 finds” slab. Put **“2 finds · 0:55 played”** on one line beneath the winner; enlarge both photos to approximately twice their current width and show the final ranking inside the modal. The celebration currently hides the useful scoreboard.

3. **Big-screen Rage — map and player labels.** Enlarge the arena to fill the available left panel, increase player labels to **18 px minimum at the supplied 800 px width**, and darken the underlying street imagery further. Players, zones, and pickups should dominate the projection.

4. **Phone hunt — competing buttons.** Rename **“Snap it” → “Take photo”** and **“Pocket” → “Pocket phone.”** Keep Take photo filled coral; make Pocket phone a smaller text action beneath it. The two large buttons currently compete.

5. **Phone gameplay — HUD contrast.** Make the score, match timer, and feed count white; retain muted text for scale markings and secondary labels. Their current gray treatment recedes too much for outdoor glances.

6. **Lobby — bottom controls.** Move **Leave squad** into a compact text action and add scroll-content bottom padding equal to the fixed Start game footer plus **16 px and the safe-area inset**. The footer currently crowds the final control.

7. **Phone results — photo layout.** Make the two photo columns fill the card’s available width equally, with a **12 px gap**. Remove repeated “Ben ·” from each caption when both belong to the named favourite; retain the hunt prompt.
