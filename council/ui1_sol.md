Most impactful fixes, in order:

1. **08 — Recompose for the projector.** Replace the thin header with a prominent phase, current hunt/escape instruction, and countdown. Use 32–48 px primary text and at least 24 px player names at the target projector resolution. Suppress street labels and enlarge players, strike zones, and the latest photo/verdict. Remove the tiny activity log.

2. **04/06 — Make the immediate objective dominate.** Move the hunt/escape card directly below the HUD; let the radar occupy the remaining space. In Rage, make **“↗ Walk 11 m”** and **“Impact in 8 s”** the largest elements. Keep match time smaller and visually separate from phase time.

3. **03/07 — Fix bottom-bar obstruction.** Add scroll padding equal to the entire fixed footer height plus 16 px and the device safe area. The big-screen controls and results awards currently disappear beneath the action bars. Give “Leave squad” a readable text-button treatment.

4. **Across screens — Strengthen secondary contrast.** Brighten instructional text, inactive segmented options, radar distances, and footer actions. Start with **02 body copy, 03 mode options, and 07 stat labels**. Preserve clear disabled states; ordinary secondary actions should remain visibly available.

5. **01/04/06/07 — Give Krillion a recurring presence.** Reuse the home mascot as a small expressive portrait beside the hunger meter, with hungry/enraged/satisfied variants. Replace the generic results wave with the mascot’s outcome expression. The competitors’ strongest advantage is recognizable character presence.

6. **07 — Lead with a satisfying report.** Put personal points and distance first, then the leaderboard; compress zero finds/dodges/revives into one row. Keep “Walked” on one line and move “Demo” into a single badge beside the report title.

7. **03 — Reduce setup competition.** Keep squad, mode, duration, and Start visible. Move arena, practice bots, and photo-sharing controls into a collapsed **Settings** section. Replace the exposed truncated spectator URL with one **Open big screen** action.

8. **02/05 — Tighten instructions and clarify pocket mode.** Reduce each how-to paragraph to one action sentence while retaining the safety/privacy information. On pocket mode, keep the object dominant, make the countdown smaller, and add a clear **Return to game** control; the current screen provides no visible exit.
