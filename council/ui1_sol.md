The previous version has energy, but its nested panels and competing controls dilute the main action. The competitor has a coherent atmosphere and focused prompts, though its tiny text and dark presentation reduce readability.

Most impactful remaining problems, in priority order:

1. **Round reveal and squad results — the reward is undersold.** Small cards and substantial empty space make the payoff feel quieter than the hunt. Enlarge the winning card, place the tier and earned points prominently beside it, and move the next-round timer directly below.

2. **Home — the next hunt is hidden behind a generic action.** “Keep hunting” doesn’t expose the unfinished task. Make the remaining “A pattern made of squares” prompt the primary tappable card, opening its camera; keep today’s progress alongside the score.

3. **Card detail and collection — rarity information is too small.** Tier, points, and discovery status are central to the game but receive metadata treatment. Enlarge those elements on the detail screen; simplify collection cards to photo, name, tier, and points. Remove the unexplained “rarity 98.”

4. **Across the app — primary action colors lack a clear rule.** Hunting and retry actions are black; starting a squad round and taking its photo are orange. Use orange consistently for the main progression action, outlined buttons for secondary actions, and reserve tier colors for rewards.

5. **Mobile landing — the decorative opening delays the pitch.** The header gap and card fan occupy roughly the first 400 pixels. Reduce that space and slightly shrink the fan so the headline and primary Play button arrive earlier. Shorten “Play in your browser” to “Play.”

6. **Rejected photo — recovery requires rereading the prompt.** “Not quite” dominates while the useful diagnosis sits underneath. Promote the actionable reason to the heading—“No square pattern visible”—retain the photo, and keep “Try again” as the clear primary action.
