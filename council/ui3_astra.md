Rarefind has stronger everyday readability. Krillion has a more cohesive visual world and a clearer progression from prompt to reward, but its tiny, low-contrast text hurts usability.

Most impactful remaining problems, in priority order:

1. **Collection — cards lose their reason for existing.** Repeated “Red standpipe” cards look interchangeable because V3 removed the prompt. Restore a compact prompt header on each card; retain the simplified name/tier/score footer.

2. **Home — the next action comes after a large empty field.** The card fan and score feel disconnected from “Next up.” Compose the fan, score and active prompt as one tighter group, with the prompt immediately beneath the score. Keep navigation anchored below.

3. **Rejected photo — exhausted attempts produce a weak exit.** “0 tries left today” leaves only a distant text link. Replace “Back” with a full-width **“Back to Today”** button when attempts are exhausted; use **“Try again”** while attempts remain. Fix “1 tries left” on Today.

4. **Card detail and squad reveal — reward information repeats.** Tier and points appear on the card and again below it; the common reveal adds another explanation of the same count. Keep tier/score on the card and one clear rarity statistic beneath it, such as **“3 of 3 finds matched yours.”**

5. **Across app — secondary information is too faint and small.** Attempt counts, rarity counts, filter text and lobby metadata are fragile for outdoor use. Increase essential metadata size and darken it; keep bright tier colors primarily in borders and badges.

6. **Mobile landing — card footers still break awkwardly.** “One of One” and “Only find” wrap into cramped stacks. Give the badge a nonwrapping row and remove the redundant “Only find” text from these promotional cards.

7. **Friends / squad screens — naming changes mid-flow.** “Friends,” “Play with friends” and “Squad” describe the same feature differently. Use **“Squad”** consistently for navigation, entry and lobby; retain **“Invite friends”** as the action.
