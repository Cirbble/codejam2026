The current safe-state card communicates more coherently, and the larger big-screen results rows are a meaningful improvement. Home, lobby, and phone results are essentially unchanged.

Most impactful remaining fixes:

1. **Rage danger + impact — direction card.** “Brace!” suggests stopping, while the retained arrow suggests moving. At impact, replace both with a brief resolved outcome: **“Hit · −1 heart”** or **“Dodged!”** During escape, ensure arrow, map bearing, and direction text share the same reference frame. On the rotating iPhone map, calculate the arrow relative to heading; on the north-up radar, “south” should point straight down.

2. **Rage impact + safe — teammate labels.** “Shrimpy” and “Barnacle” overlap markers and each other. Add collision-aware label placement with an **8 px minimum gap**, prioritize “You,” and connect displaced labels to their markers with short leader lines. Apply the same rule on the projector.

3. **Big-screen Rage — arena composition.** The dark, blurry street imagery competes with a relatively small arena. Enlarge the arena to fill the area below the header and left of the scoreboard; use a subdued, simplified map beneath it. Give player names consistent opaque backplates.

4. **Hunt — repeated objective.** The top “Krillion craves: Something red” banner duplicates the bottom card. Remove that duplicate; keep **“Something red” + countdown + actions** together at the bottom. Shorten its eyebrow to **“Round 1.”**

5. **Phone results — photo hierarchy.** “Krillion’s Favourite · Ben” sits above two equally weighted photos, making the favourite unclear. Mark the actual favourite directly on its photo with a small crown; remove the detached heading and **“GRASS REPORT.”**

6. **Gameplay — secondary contrast and symbols.** Radar distances and depleted hearts are faint. Brighten those functional cues, and replace emoji/text-based sound, trophy, and arrow symbols with one consistent icon set. Preserve the illustrated mascot.

7. **Lobby — scattered secondary actions.** Place **Share big screen** and **Test sound** in one compact utility group. Move **Leave squad** into the header as a back action, away from the fixed **Start game** button.
