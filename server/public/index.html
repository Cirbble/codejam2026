squall

A GPS game for friends. Storm cells form where you stand, and your phone tells you which way to walk before they land.

Play in browser Android iPhone

Walk out

The cell heats up from green to magenta. Be outside it when it lands.

Revive

Three hits and you are down until a teammate walks over to you.

Outlast it

Anyone still standing when the storm passes wins it for the crew.
